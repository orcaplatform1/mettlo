REVISION 6 SUMMARY (decisions made this round)
------------------------------------------------------------
- RELEASE: all modules ship together in ONE single release (no phased rollout).
- ADMIN ROLES: FINANCE role NOT created. All finance (payments, refunds, invoices, creator
  earnings, commissions, chargebacks) is SUPER_ADMIN only. Full role matrix: section 63.
- ACCOUNT DELETION: members and creators can request deletion of their own account from
  inside the app/web; retention rules defined (section 57).
- SANCTIONS: members or creators who behave unethically can be temporarily suspended or
  permanently banned (section 57).
- COACH DATA ACCESS: each coach sees ALL data a member generates in that coach's own area
  (their programs, sessions, challenges, coaching, bookings, live attendance). Health data
  (Apple Health / Health Connect, weight, measurements, progress) is visible to EVERY coach the
  member is subscribed to, based on the member's explicit consent (sections 14, 40).
- CREATORS: creators are NOT companies and are engaged under Mettlo like in-house service
  providers. The platform collects NO IBAN, TCKN or tax number and has NO payout module.
  An internal creator earnings ledger is kept for reporting only (sections 21, 26).
  iyzico is used as a standard single merchant (Mettlo), no marketplace / split payment.
- AGE: 18+ only. Under-18 users may join ONLY when registered by a parent / legal guardian
  (section 58).
- LEGAL TEXTS: drafting deferred to the end of the build, list kept in section 62.
- CHECKOUT / CAYMA: access opens at the moment of payment, so digital purchases carry no
  right of withdrawal; checkout shows this and requires acknowledgement (section 60).
- CREATOR EXIT: a leaving creator must indemnify the payments they received in full; if not,
  legal action will be taken. Every active subscriber of a leaving creator gets ONE free
  subscription to a creator of their choice (section 59).
- HOSTING: servers in Turkiye (Turhost). Personal and sensitive data stays in Turkiye
  (section 61).
- IYS: Mettlo registers with IYS (Ileti Yonetim Sistemi) for commercial SMS / e-mail (section 61).
- PROVIDERS: lowest-cost option chosen for each provider (section 61).
- LIVE CREDITS: 1/3 of credit revenue goes to the coach, 2/3 to Mettlo. Premium Live tier
  defined. Live cap wording resolved (section 39).
- COMMERCE: products are sold ONLY by Mettlo. Creators do not sell, recommend for
  commission or run affiliate links. Creator store / creator commerce / affiliate removed
  (sections 18-20, 33).
- COMMISSION: flat 20% everywhere (the only exception is live credits, 1/3 to coach).
- DOCUMENT FIXES: wrong section references and stale "pending / not decided" labels corrected.

8. MULTI-BRANCH PROGRAM & CLASS ENGINE
============================================================

PRINCIPLE
------------------------------------------------------------
Mettlo is not fitness-only. A member can join fitness, yoga, pilates, dance, nutrition
coaching and other branches on the same account, subscribe to creators from different
branches, and take their classes. "Fitness" is one branch among several, not the
platform itself.

BRANCH (admin-managed, extensible without code changes)
------------------------------------------------------------
- branch: fitness / strength, yoga, pilates, dance, HIIT & cardio, mobility & stretching,
  meditation & breathwork, nutrition & healthy eating (dietitians / nutrition coaches),
  and others added over time
- allowed content types
- session template (which block types are enabled: strength / timed-flow / cardio / free)
- level scale (e.g. beginner / intermediate / advanced, or branch-specific)
- metric set (steps, calories, duration, heart rate ... which metrics matter for the branch)
- equipment list (optional)
- creator requirements (credential / verification level per branch)
- commerce categories linked to the branch (optional)

RULES
------------------------------------------------------------
- Exercise fields such as sets / reps / weight / muscle group are OPTIONAL per branch.
- Discovery, search filters, recommendations and analytics are branch-aware.
- A creator can belong to more than one branch.
- Naming in code and UI: use "Content" / "Class" / "Session" / "Program", not "Workout"
  as the umbrella term.

NUTRITION / HEALTHY-EATING BRANCH (dietitians and healthy-eating program coaches)
------------------------------------------------------------
- Credential verification is mandatory for professional titles (e.g. dietitian).
- Health and nutrition advice may be regulated: legal / KVKK review is required BEFORE
  this branch opens to creators and members.
- Keep clear separation between general healthy-eating content and individual medical
  advice.
- [OPEN] exact professional rules to be confirmed with a legal advisor.

13. LIVE ENGINE
============================================================

- Live scheduling
- Live room
- Participant management
- Chat
- Moderation
- Attendance
- Replay
- Notifications

LIVE TYPES
------------------------------------------------------------
- Member Live (subscriber-only)
- Paid Live
- Coaching Live
(No public "Free Live" type: every live session requires an active entitlement.)

LIVE MODES
------------------------------------------------------------
Mettlo supports TWO live delivery modes, chosen per session by the creator based on
audience size and need:

A) IN-PLATFORM (WebRTC / LiveKit)
   - COACH LIVE: the coach streams to their subscribers. Members watch, chat and react.
     Member camera and microphone are OFF by default (viewer-only).
   - INTERACTIVE CLASS: small group (yoga, pilates, group coaching). Member camera is
     optional (default off), microphone muted by default. The coach can see members and
     correct posture. Capacity cap per class (recommended 20-30).
   - 1:1 SESSION: real-time coaching call (see sections 22 and 24).
   - Runs on Mettlo's own infrastructure; join access, entitlement checks, recording and
     replay are fully controlled by Mettlo (see section 39 for cost model).

B) EXTERNAL LINK (coach's own Zoom or similar account)
   - For large subscriber broadcasts where in-platform WebRTC cost would be high (see
     section 39). Zero live-infrastructure cost to Mettlo; the coach pays for their own
     Zoom (or equivalent) plan if needed.
   - Access control: the session requires REGISTRATION on the external platform. Mettlo
     registers only members with an active entitlement for that creator, using the
     provider's registration API, and receives back a per-member, unique join link.
     Mettlo shows that unique link only to the entitled member; it is never posted as a
     public or shared link.
   - This is best-effort protection, not a hard guarantee: a registered member could
     still forward their personal link to someone else. This limitation is disclosed to
     creators when they choose this mode.
   - Recording: the coach records the session on their own account and manually uploads
     the recording file to Mettlo afterward (via the creator dashboard). Mettlo then
     stores, transcodes and serves the replay under the normal signed-playback and
     entitlement rules, like any other video content.
   - The coach is responsible for keeping the recording focused on their own camera /
     screen where possible, since member video may be captured by the external tool.

ACCESS & CONSENT (applies to both modes)
------------------------------------------------------------
- Only members with an active entitlement for that creator / class can request to join
  (section 23).
- Immediately before the join link (in-platform token or external unique link) is
  issued, the member is shown a mandatory consent screen describing: that the session
  is live, that their camera/microphone (if enabled) may be visible to the coach and
  other participants where applicable, and how the recording (if any) will be stored
  and used. The member must read and accept this screen; the link is issued only after
  acceptance.
- This acceptance is treated as the member's KVKK consent for that specific live
  session, timestamped and logged (kvkk_consents, tied to the session).
- Non-subscribers cannot obtain a join link in either mode.

HOSTING DEVICES
------------------------------------------------------------
- The coach can go live from a phone (iOS / Android app), desktop web or tablet web, in
  either mode.
- Pre-live check (in-platform mode): camera / mic test, network quality indicator,
  low-bandwidth fallback (lower resolution, audio-only).

QUALITY AND COST CONTROLS (in-platform mode)
------------------------------------------------------------
- Default viewer quality 480p (about 0.7 Mbps). The coach publishes with simulcast so
  each viewer receives an adaptive layer. Higher quality only where needed.
- Members subscribe only to the coach's video and a limited set of thumbnails, not to a
  full grid.
- Audio-only fallback for weak networks.
- Per-room participant cap and per-creator monthly live-minute limit (section 39).

RECORDING AND PRIVACY (KVKK)
------------------------------------------------------------
- In-platform mode: only the coach's stream (video, audio, shared screen) is recorded
  for replay by default. Member video and audio are NOT recorded by default.
- External-link mode: recording is manual (coach uploads); see above for guidance on
  keeping member video out of the recording where possible.
- Replay follows the same entitlement rules as the live session.
- Recording retention is set per plan (storage cost applies).
- Live chat retention and moderation follow section 37 (moderation).

LIVE DATA
------------------------------------------------------------
- live_sessions, live_participants, live_messages, live_replays
- live_mode (in_platform / external_link), live_external_registrations
- live_usage (participant minutes, estimated GB), live_limits, live_credit_packs
  (section 39)

HEALTH PRINCIPLE
------------------------------------------------------------
User explicitly grants device permissions (Apple Health / Health Connect).
Health data is special-category personal data under KVKK (ozel nitelikli kisisel veri):
sharing it with a coach requires the member's EXPLICIT consent, separate from the terms of
service.
Admins do not access individual health data (aggregate only, section 63).
All health data is stored in Turkiye (section 61).

COACH DATA ACCESS (DECIDED, Rev 6)
------------------------------------------------------------
1. OWN-AREA DATA (always visible to the coach while the subscription is active):
   everything the member does in that coach's area — enrolled programs and session logs,
   workouts / sets / reps, challenge participation and progress, coaching check-ins, notes,
   messages, bookings, attendance, live attendance.
   A coach NEVER sees program / session / challenge details the member does in ANOTHER
   coach's area.
2. HEALTH DATA (visible to EVERY coach the member is subscribed to):
   steps, distance, active calories, exercise duration, heart rate, sleep, weight,
   measurements, progress photos, activity trends.
3. CONSENT FLOW: when a member subscribes to a coach, a separate consent screen asks:
   "Saglik verilerimi (adim, uyku, nabiz, kilo, olculer, ilerleme fotograflari) bu koc ile
   paylasiyorum." Consent is per coach, timestamped, stored in health_share_consents.
   Without consent the coach sees only own-area data.
4. REVOCATION: the member can revoke health sharing per coach at any time from privacy
   settings; the coach immediately loses access to health data but keeps own-area data.
5. END OF SUBSCRIPTION: coach access to that member's health data ends when the
   subscription ends; own-area history stays readable to the coach for 30 days (proposed)
   and then becomes hidden.
6. SAFEGUARDS: view-only in the coach workspace, no bulk export / download, every coach access
   to health data is written to audit logs; confidentiality obligation in the creator
   agreement (section 62).
7. MINORS: health sharing for a minor requires the guardian's consent (section 58).
8. APPLE / GOOGLE: HealthKit / Health Connect data is shared only for the member's own
   training / health purpose, never for advertising (store health-data rules).

WHAT THE STORE RULES SAY (researched 2026-09-25, VERIFY BEFORE LAUNCH)

23. ENTITLEMENT & ACCESS CONTROL (DECIDED)
============================================================

CORE RULE
------------------------------------------------------------
Access to paid content is granted ONLY by the system, never by a creator's manual
action. A creator is a content manager / coach: they cannot grant themselves or others
paid access. Off-platform payment (WhatsApp, cash, IBAN) does not unlock content.

ENTITLEMENT SOURCES (the only ways access can start)
------------------------------------------------------------
1. payment_succeeded    - iyzico webhook confirmed
2. store_receipt        - Apple / Google server notification validated
3. free                 - content marked FREE / MEMBERS_ONLY per rules
4. promo_trial          - platform-defined trials and coupons
5. creator_invite_grant - one-time declared quota, logged complimentary access (below)
6. admin_grant          - support / compensation, always with a reason and audit log
7. creator_exit_gift    - one free subscription when a member's creator leaves (section 59)

ENTITLEMENT LIFECYCLE
------------------------------------------------------------
active -> grace (failed payment) -> expired
active -> paused
active -> cancelled (access until period end)
active -> revoked (refund / chargeback / abuse / permanent ban)
active -> suspended (creator suspended: billing paused for the suspension period, section 57)
Every state change writes an entitlement_events record.

WHY COACHES STILL HAVE WHAT THEY NEED (so they have no reason to go off-platform)
------------------------------------------------------------
- Payment link: creator shares a checkout link / QR / coupon on Instagram or WhatsApp;
  the member pays through Mettlo and access unlocks automatically; the sale is
  attributed to the creator.
- Creator invite grants (DECIDED DESIGN): during creator onboarding, the platform asks "How many
  active students do you currently have?" with a visible notice next to the field, e.g. "Registering
  them on Mettlo will not be charged." The number the creator declares becomes their LIFETIME invite
  quota (a one-time cap set at signup, not a recurring monthly allowance) — the system will not let
  the creator send more free invites than the number they declared. This exists specifically to stop
  a creator from grandfathering their real existing students honestly, then later bringing new,
  never-before-served customers in for free by taking payment off-platform. The system can only
  enforce the declared CAP; it has no way to verify the declared number itself, so this still relies
  on the creator's honesty for the initial figure.
  - Each individual invite grant lasts between 1 and 25 days, chosen by the creator per invite;
    grants of 26-30 days are NOT allowed (hard validation in the invite form).
  - The moment an invited person signs up through the invite, the system automatically assigns them
    a free-subscriber role/status for that creator — no manual admin step.
  - An admin can raise a creator's declared quota later (e.g. verified growth), logged with a reason.
- Coaching workspace, check-ins, progress and messaging live in Mettlo, so the value
  stays inside the platform even if the first contact happened on WhatsApp.
- Commission is flat for all sales regardless of source (DECIDED, section 21/53): no lower rate for
  customers the creator brought in themselves versus customers found through Mettlo discovery.

CONTROLS
------------------------------------------------------------
- Terms of service: paid coaching and content must be sold through Mettlo.
- Fraud signals: creator self-subscription, many invite grants to one device,
  off-platform handle sharing in messages (moderation assist), a creator who exhausts their
  declared invite quota unusually fast, or invite grants clustered at exactly the maximum 25-day
  length (possible attempt to disguise a recurring free arrangement as a series of "trials").
- Sensitive: entitlement data and health data are separate systems (KVKK).

39. LIVE COST MODEL, LIMITS & INFRASTRUCTURE DECISIONS
============================================================

DECISION
------------------------------------------------------------
- In-platform live = WebRTC on LiveKit Cloud. No HLS / CDN broadcast mode.
- Large subscriber broadcasts use the EXTERNAL LINK mode instead (section 13), which
  has zero live-infrastructure cost to Mettlo.
- Viewer-only members in in-platform mode still cost bandwidth (they receive the
  coach's video), so in-platform live must be limited and priced. It cannot be an
  unlimited free benefit.

LIVEKIT CLOUD PRICING (researched 2026-09-25 from public comparison sites, VERIFY on
the official page)
------------------------------------------------------------
- Build: free (5,000 WebRTC minutes, 50 GB downstream). Development only.
- Ship: $50 / month. 150,000 WebRTC minutes and 250 GB downstream included, then
  $0.12 / GB.
- Scale: $500 / month. 3 TB downstream included, then $0.10 / GB.
- WebRTC participant minute: about $0.0004 - $0.0005.
- Video recording / composite export: about $0.015 per minute.
- Upstream (coach / members sending) is free. Downstream (to viewers) is billed and is
  the main cost driver.
- Plans may limit concurrent rooms / participants. [VERIFY before launch]

COST PER VIEWER-HOUR, IN-PLATFORM MODE (bandwidth per viewer is an ASSUMPTION; measure
in real tests)
------------------------------------------------------------
Formula: GB per hour x $0.12 + 60 minutes x $0.0005 (Ship rates).
| Viewer quality        | GB / hour | Cost / viewer-hour | In TL (49 TL per USD, example rate) |
|-------------------------|-----------|----------------------|----------------------------------------|
| 720p, about 1.5 Mbps    | 0.68      | about $0.11          | about 5.4 TL                           |
| 480p, about 0.7 Mbps    | 0.32      | about $0.07          | about 3.3 TL                           |
Included Ship quotas are small: 250 GB covers only about 370 viewer-hours at 720p.

- If every creator used in-platform mode for large broadcasts, cost would grow
  linearly and consume most of the commission (before payment fees, store fees, KDV,
  support and other costs). External-link mode removes this cost entirely for
  broadcasts; in-platform mode should be reserved for genuinely interactive small
  classes and 1:1 sessions, where the cost is naturally bounded by room-size limits.

PEAK CONCURRENCY (in-platform mode)
------------------------------------------------------------
- Evening peaks with many creators live at once: check Cloud concurrency limits.
- Self-host: capacity has to be built for the peak hour.

SELF-HOST OPTION (LiveKit server is open source)
------------------------------------------------------------
- No per-minute / per-GB fee to LiveKit. You pay for servers, bandwidth, TURN, Redis
  (multi-node), recording (Egress) and operations.
- Reported infrastructure figures [VERIFY]: bare-metal bandwidth about $0.001 / GB
  (Hetzner), a 16-core server about EUR 39 / month, about 200 concurrent users per
  roughly $60 node as a starting estimate (fewer for video-heavy rooms).
- With those numbers bandwidth becomes almost free, so cost moves to fixed server
  capacity and engineering time. Servers in the EU are closer to Turkiye, but media
  still leaves Turkiye (KVKK check).
- Trigger to evaluate: monthly in-platform live bill of several hundred USD or more, or
  Cloud limits blocking growth -> run a load test and a cost model, then decide.

LIVE PRICING / LIMIT MODEL (DECIDED)
------------------------------------------------------------
1. PER-CREATOR IN-PLATFORM LIVE CAP (decided): 5 sessions per week, 1 hour each, 16 hours per month
   hard cap. This applies to in-platform (LiveKit) usage only — external-link broadcasts are not
   counted against this cap, since they cost Mettlo nothing. Note: 5h/week over a typical month is
   arithmetically closer to 20-22 hours; 16 hours/month is the stated hard ceiling regardless, so in
   practice a creator will hit the monthly cap before using every weekly session in some months —
   flag this for the product team to confirm which number should govern in the UI (weekly 5x1h or
   the 16h/month ceiling) so the limit is enforced consistently.
   RESOLVED (Rev 6): the 16 hours / month ceiling GOVERNS. Weekly limit is max 5 sessions of max
   1 hour each. The UI shows "remaining in-platform live hours this month"; when the monthly 16
   hours are used up, no new in-platform session can be scheduled until the next month (the
   creator can still use external-link mode).
2. LIVE CREDIT PRICING (proposed by Claude, as requested):
   - 1 live credit = access to one interactive class booking, up to 60 minutes.
   - Price: 29 TL per credit. At 480p (about 3.3 TL infra cost/hour) this is roughly 9x the raw
     infrastructure cost; at 720p (about 5.4 TL/hour) roughly 5x — a healthy margin over the LiveKit
     cost even before payment fees, so it comfortably covers infra plus overhead.
   - Packs (bound to ONE creator, per section 24): 4 credits for 99 TL (~24.75 TL/credit), 8 credits
     for 179 TL (~22.4 TL/credit) — a modest volume discount that still clears the cost floor.
   - Interactive-class access is credit-based by default; membership does NOT include free
     interactive-class hours unless a creator or admin chooses to bundle credits into a higher-tier
     membership as a merchandising decision later (not a core system requirement).
   - Credits expire after 90 days (proposed; adjust if needed) and follow the payment-channel rules
     in section 22 (conservatively IAP / Play Billing on mobile).
   - REVENUE SPLIT (DECIDED, Rev 6): 1/3 of credit revenue goes to the coach, 2/3 to Mettlo
     (Mettlo's share carries the LiveKit infrastructure cost). Computed on the net amount after
     store fee where applicable. Example on web: 29 TL credit -> about 9.67 TL coach, about
     19.33 TL Mettlo (KDV excluded). This is the ONLY exception to the flat 20% commission.
   - PREMIUM LIVE MEMBERSHIP TIER (defined, Rev 6): an optional higher membership tier a creator
     can switch on. It = the creator's normal membership + 4 interactive-class credits per month
     (credits valid only for that creator, reset monthly, unused credits do not roll over).
     Minimum add-on price over the base membership: 99 TL / month (same as the 4-credit pack).
     Split: base membership part flat 20% commission; add-on part 1/3 coach, 2/3 Mettlo.
3. Room caps: interactive classes 20-30 participants; coach-live (in-platform) rooms sized by plan.
4. Default 480p for viewers, simulcast, audio-only fallback, viewers subscribe only to the coach
   track.
5. Usage metering: live_usage per session (participant minutes, estimated GB) feeds the 16-hour cap,
   analytics and margin reporting per creator.
6. External-link mode has no Mettlo infrastructure cost and is not subject to the 16-hour cap; the
   platform commission model for external-link sessions is the same flat 20% as any other paid
   content (section 53).
7. LIVE STRATEGY CONFIRMED: continue with LiveKit for in-platform interactive classes / 1:1 sessions,
   and Zoom (external-link mode, section 13) for large subscriber broadcasts, combined with the
   credit system above. Self-hosting LiveKit remains a later option only if the in-platform bill
   grows large despite the 16-hour cap (see the self-host trigger above).

57. ACCOUNT LIFECYCLE: DELETION & SANCTIONS
============================================================

ACCOUNT DELETION (member and creator, self-service)
------------------------------------------------------------
- Members and creators can request deletion of their own account from settings on web, iOS
  and Android (Apple requires in-app account deletion).
- Before confirming, the user can download a data export (KVKK).
- 30-day grace period: the account is set to pending_deletion, login shows "cancel deletion".
  After 30 days deletion runs automatically (worker: account-deletion).
- Subscriptions: web subscriptions are cancelled immediately, no refund for digital services
  (section 60). App Store / Google Play subscriptions must be cancelled by the user in the
  store; the app shows how.
- CREATOR accounts: a creator deletion request first starts the CREATOR EXIT process
  (section 59); the account is deleted only after the exit is closed.

WHAT IS DELETED
- profile, avatar, health data, health connections, progress photos, measurements,
  nutrition logs, device tokens, notification preferences, private files.
- messages and community posts: content deleted, sender shown as "Silinmis kullanici".
- reviews: kept for rating integrity but anonymized.

WHAT IS KEPT (legal obligation, access SUPER_ADMIN only)
- invoices, payments, orders, refunds, entitlement events, KVKK consent logs, checkout
  acknowledgements, sanction records, audit logs — kept for the legal retention period
  (VUK / TTK; exact periods confirmed by the accountant, section 26), then destroyed.
- identity fields in kept records are minimized to what the legal record requires.

SANCTIONS (members and creators)
------------------------------------------------------------
Types:
- warning
- temporary suspension: 1 to 90 days (chosen per case)
- permanent ban

Reasons (examples): harassment, hate speech, sexual content, fraud / payment abuse, taking
payment off-platform, fake reviews / engagement, sharing paid content, ban evasion, unsafe
advice, any conduct against the terms of service.

Who can apply:
- MODERATOR: warning, suspension up to 7 days
- ADMIN: suspension up to 90 days, permanent ban
- SUPER_ADMIN: everything, and lifting any sanction

Rules:
- Every sanction stores reason, evidence, issuer, start / end, and writes an audit log.
- The user is notified with the reason and end date; one appeal via support, reviewed by
  someone other than the issuer.
- SUSPENDED MEMBER: cannot log in during the suspension; subscriptions keep running, no refund
  (terms of service).
- BANNED MEMBER: entitlements revoked, no refund (terms of service); hashed e-mail / phone /
  device identifiers kept in banned_identifiers to block re-registration.
- SUSPENDED CREATOR: profile hidden, no new sales, lives and bookings cancelled with credits
  returned; subscribers' billing is PAUSED for the suspension period so members are not charged
  for time without service.
- Creator suspended longer than 30 days, or permanently banned: creator exit rules apply
  (section 59), including indemnity and the subscriber gift.

TABLES: account_sanctions, account_deletion_requests, banned_identifiers

58. AGE LIMIT & GUARDIAN (PARENT) ACCOUNTS
============================================================

- Mettlo is for users 18 and older. Birth date is required at signup.
- Users under 18 CANNOT register themselves. They can join ONLY when a parent / legal
  guardian registers them: the guardian creates (or uses) their own adult account and adds a
  minor profile linked to it (guardian_links).
- The guardian gives all consents for the minor (KVKK, health sharing with coaches, terms) and
  makes all purchases (guardian_consents).
- Proposed minimum age even with a guardian: 13 (confirm with lawyer).
- Safeguards for minor profiles (proposed):
  - coach <-> minor direct messages are visible to the guardian
  - 1:1 live sessions with a minor only after guardian approval per booking
  - progress photos disabled for minors
  - shown with a nickname only on public leaderboards / community
- Signup screens and terms state clearly: "18 yasindan kucukler yalnizca ebeveyn / yasal
  vasi kaydi ile uye olabilir."

TABLES: guardian_links, guardian_consents

59. CREATOR EXIT
============================================================

Applies when a creator leaves voluntarily, requests account deletion, is suspended longer
than 30 days, or is permanently banned.

CREATOR OBLIGATIONS (creator agreement)
------------------------------------------------------------
- Voluntary exit requires 30 days' written notice from the creator dashboard (proposed).
- A leaving creator is obliged to indemnify (tazmin) Mettlo IN FULL for the payments they
  received, as defined in the creator agreement.
- If the indemnity is not paid, Mettlo will take legal action (dava) against the creator.
- [OPEN - lawyer] exact wording, scope and enforceability of the clause; the signed creator
  agreement must hold the creator's legal identity so legal action is possible.

SUBSCRIBER PROTECTION
------------------------------------------------------------
- Every member with an ACTIVE subscription to the leaving creator receives ONE free
  subscription (one month) to ANY creator of their choice (entitlement source
  creator_exit_gift, section 23).
- The member has 60 days to choose the creator; after that the gift expires (proposed).
- Annual subscribers: the remaining full months are also converted into free months with the
  chosen creator (proposed).
- The chosen creator earns their normal share of their web list price for gifted months in
  the earnings ledger; the cost is funded by Mettlo and claimed from the leaving creator's
  indemnity (proposed).
- Programs already purchased stay accessible to buyers; the creator agreement grants Mettlo a
  licence to keep serving already-sold content (proposed).
- Future bookings are cancelled; unused live credits of that creator are refunded (web) or
  replaced with the same number of credits for the chosen creator (proposed).
- Members are notified by push, e-mail and in-app.

TABLES: creator_exits, creator_exit_gifts

60. CHECKOUT & RIGHT OF WITHDRAWAL (CAYMA)
============================================================

- Digital purchases (memberships, programs, paid content, live credits, Premium Live tier):
  access opens at the moment of payment, so the service is considered performed immediately
  and there is NO right of withdrawal (Mesafeli Sozlesmeler Yonetmeligi exception for
  services performed instantly in electronic form).
- Web checkout, before the pay button: the pre-information form (on bilgilendirme formu) and
  the distance sales contract (mesafeli satis sozlesmesi) are shown, plus a mandatory
  checkbox:
  "Satin aldigim dijital icerik / hizmete erisimin odeme ile aninda baslayacagini ve bu
  nedenle cayma hakkimin bulunmadigini biliyor ve kabul ediyorum."
- The acknowledgement is stored with timestamp, user, product, contract version
  (checkout_acknowledgements).
- Physical products from the Mettlo Store: the normal 14-day right of withdrawal and return
  flow applies.
- Booked future classes / 1:1 sessions: follow the cancellation policy of section 24.
- App Store / Google Play purchases: refunds are handled by Apple / Google under their rules.
  [DISCUSS LATER - see section 41: whether this approach causes any problem for the stores.]

HOSTING (DECIDED)
------------------------------------------------------------
- Turhost servers in Turkiye for API, workers, PostgreSQL, Redis and private file storage.

PROVIDERS (DECIDED: lowest cost per category; VERIFY current prices at build time)
------------------------------------------------------------
- Language / stack: TypeScript everywhere (NestJS API + workers, Next.js web apps, React
  Native + Expo mobile, Prisma ORM), same as the founder's existing ORCA stack.
- Private file storage: self-hosted S3-compatible storage on the Turhost server (no extra
  licence cost).
- On-demand video (creator content, replays) + CDN: Bunny Stream / Bunny CDN (transcoding
  included, pay per storage and delivery). Creator videos are the creator's own content;
  the creator agreement covers processing of the creator's image abroad.
- Video transcoding: done by Bunny Stream (no own FFmpeg farm needed).
- E-mail: Amazon SES.
- SMS: Turkish SMS provider with built-in IYS integration, chosen by lowest quote.
- Push: FCM + APNs through Expo push service (free).
- Search: PostgreSQL full-text search with Turkish configuration (no extra service).
- Queue: BullMQ on the existing Redis.
- Error monitoring: Sentry free tier (or self-hosted alternative if the limit is exceeded).
- e-Fatura / e-Arsiv integrator: lowest per-invoice quote.
- Payment: iyzico standard merchant (section 22). Live: LiveKit Cloud + Zoom (section 39).

IYS (DECIDED)
------------------------------------------------------------
- Mettlo registers with IYS (Ileti Yonetim Sistemi) before launch.
- Commercial SMS / e-mail (campaigns, promotions) only to users with a recorded IYS consent;

  commercial consent.

63. ADMIN ROLE & PERMISSION MATRIX
============================================================

SUPER_ADMIN (1-2 people, 2FA mandatory)
- Everything, including: role management, system settings, ALL FINANCE (payments, refunds
  approval, chargebacks, invoices, commissions, creator earnings ledger), KVKK requests, legal
  retention records, lifting any sanction, raising creator invite quotas.

ADMIN (2FA mandatory)
- Users, creators, content, programs, challenges, branches, bookings, reviews, campaigns,
  coupons, Mettlo Store products / orders (no payment details), creator success, analytics
  (aggregate), sanctions up to 90 days and permanent ban, creator exits (operations part).
- Can REQUEST refunds; approval is SUPER_ADMIN.
- No finance data, no individual health data.

MODERATOR (2FA mandatory)
- Content, community, reviews, reports, creator verification documents, warnings and
  suspensions up to 7 days.
- No finance data, no health data, only minimal user info needed for the case.

SUPPORT (2FA mandatory)
- User lookup with masked e-mail / phone, subscription and entitlement status, order status,
  tickets, account deletion requests (can see and help, cannot force-delete).
- Can REQUEST refunds (approval SUPER_ADMIN).
- No finance data, no health data, no messages content.

ALL ROLES
- No admin role sees individual health data (aggregate only).
- Every access to sensitive data and every admin action is written to audit logs.
- No "log in as user" feature (proposed); if ever added, read-only and fully logged.
